The village

    Werewolf

    You, any friends you invite, and AI players, each with a secret role. Two werewolves kill one player a night; the seer checks one; the witch has a potion that saves and one that kills. By day everyone speaks in turn, then votes one player out. The village wins when the wolves are dead; the wolves, when they are as many as everyone else.

      Use your own key

      The AI players run on Claude Haiku 5.5, on Ponjo. With your own key, they run on the model you pick.

      Model

      Your key stays in this browser. While you play, your table on Ponjo's server holds it in memory only, never stored and never shown to anyone at the table, to call api.anthropic.com for your AI players. Each AI player's turn is one call billed to your key.
