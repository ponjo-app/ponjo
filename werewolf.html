The village

    Werewolf

    You and AI players, each with a secret role. Two werewolves kill one player a night; the seer checks one; the witch has a potion that saves and one that kills. By day everyone speaks in turn, then votes one player out. The village wins when the wolves are dead; the wolves, when they are as many as everyone else.

    Players

    Use your own key

    The AI players run on Claude Haiku 5.5, on Ponjo. With your own key, they run on the model you pick.

    Model

    Your key stays in this browser and goes only to api.anthropic.com, never to a Ponjo server. Each AI player's turn is one call billed to your key.
