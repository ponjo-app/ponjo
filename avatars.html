Avatars

Everyone has a face, drawn from their name by web/faces.js, so it is the same on every machine and nobody picks or uploads one. The faces below are drawn by that code.

Buddies: a body, a face and a colour

A buddy is one of five bodies (circle, square, triangle, pentagon, cloud) with a face: eyes (dot, big, happy, sleepy, squint), a mouth (smile, grin, o, flat, cat) and maybe one extra (blush or glasses). Its colour is one of eight OKLCH hues, evenly spaced, sunflower first. Outlines are a little uneven and tilted, as if drawn by hand, and the same every time.

The app's mark

The app icon and the document icon are drawings, build/icon.svg and build/document.svg, not faces: no buddy's name or face changes them. npm run icon:desktop makes the desktop icons and the wordmark from them.

People

People are always round, and wear a hat that says who they are on the project: the owner a crown, a teammate a top hat. A hat is a flat silhouette: the crown gold, the top hat in the text colour, so it reads on any page, light or dark. It sits on top of the head, so a person is as big as a buddy beside them. Their face comes from their name, the same way as a buddy's, and the hat from their id, so it stays when they rename themselves. In a doc, people take the colours its buddies left, so no one there shares a colour while eight or fewer are in it; the buddies keep theirs, since their edits show in them.

Sizes

Set by --size on .pj-avatar. Features and hats are drawn to read at 18px; a new eye, mouth or hat must too. Below 24px a face is plain: no blush or glasses, and upright.

States

Everyone shows what they are doing with their face, never with a badge or a dot, and the same on every face of them: in the header, on their card and on the thread a buddy works on. Buddies and people have the same three states, and a buddy one moment:

  • Working: a buddy running, or a person moving or typing in the doc in the last few seconds (while their cursor shows their name). The face grows a little, its body rolls and its face bobs, and its eyes turn focused, under brows that slope to the middle. The eyes show it even when nothing moves.
  • Ready (a person: here): as drawn, still. A buddy answers when you @mention it; a person is in this doc.
  • Away: greyed out. A buddy does not answer in this doc; a person is on another doc of the project, or not on it at all.
  • Failed, a moment: when a run fails, its face shakes and flashes red, once. The thread says what happened.

A finished run has no moment of its own: its edits light up in the text, in its colour. A face in the header you point at grows a little, and does nothing else. With reduced motion, faces keep their size and nothing moves.

The header

The header has one queue of faces: everyone in the doc, people and buddies alike, then you, then the Team button. Every face in it is the same button at 32px: pointing at it grows it and shows its name card, a click opens its card, and it shows its state. The queue is ordered by state: who is working first, then who is here, then who is away. Among those working, who started first leads; among the rest, whoever changed state last. So a face moves only when its state changes, and slides to its new place. Your own face stays at the end, still.

Faces have their own palette and gradient in faces.js, the one place outside the tokens with colour literals. Ink for features is #1f1d24.
