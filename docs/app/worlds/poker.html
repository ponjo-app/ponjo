Poker table

The poker page (preview/poker.js): No-Limit Hold'em with the opener, AI players (PERSONAS), friends who join by a link, a dealer and chat: a world of members on the runtime (preview/poker-world.js), stored in a room where the AI seats, dealer and clock run.

State

Table { players, slots, names, hand, handNo, button, topUp, joining, leaving,
        chat, reactions, lang, seats: { id → { thoughts: [{ hand, street, text, action }] } } }
Line  { id, by, as, text, to (whom it names), depth, about, hand, at }
hand  as in preview/poker-dealer.js, with no deck

thoughts is the seat's state, written by apply from its own runs. The answer ledger (answered, tries, why) is the runtime wrapper's, in the state. A visitor's bankroll is their browser's, apart from the table. lang is the table's language, en or zh: the room sets it from the opener's page (rooms); the AI seats speak it. Each page draws the table in its own language, the dealer's lines too.

Store

The room holds { rev, state } and gives each member only read(member): people's pages, AI seats, the dealer, the clock. wait(member, rev) returns once that member's view changed. commit sets by from the member's token and stamps at. A change is { by, at, as, base, answers, body }; base of a move is { hand, n }, the turn it was made on. No page, the opener's included, can read another seat's cards. past is unused: the hand and the chat are in the state. There are no effects.

World

view(state, member, past)    → { content, actions, tasks, seen, rules }
apply(state, change, past)   → { state, effects: [] } | { refused: why }
fallback(state, task, at)    → state         a check, else a fold; for other tasks, nothing
tries                        3

One view for every seat:

  • content: the table, this hand's actions and reactions, the last 24 lines; its own hole cards, others' at a showdown; for people, the AI seats' cards and thoughts once the hand is over; for an AI seat, its own thoughts this hand, and why its last try was refused.
  • actions: on its turn fold, check, call, bet, raise, allin, with legal amounts; say, react; people's sit, topup, leave; the opener's next, add, remove, invite, turnAway, lang.
  • seen: all presence, to all. rules: RULES, a line to speak the table's language if it is Chinese, and a persona's style.

line:<id> an AI seat the line names ask depth < DEPTH (2) its people —
line:<id> one AI seat, for a person's line to the whole table ask as above the person —
react:<hand> the winner and one other still in, if AI turn a big hand ended — —
deal:<handNo> the dealer turn, or ask after next the opener sat, or chose next the opener —
board:<hand>:<street> the dealer turn a street closed, two or more still in — —
timeout:<hand>:<n> the clock turn a turn is live — the turn's at

depth is 0 for a person's line or the rules', else one more than the agent line's. An agent's line carries on the from of the tasks its run answered. DEPTH is the table's rule. An AI seat at the table that has said no line has a hello:<id> ask, from when it sits down, a hand on or not; one the opener asked to leave, a bye:<id>:<deals> ask until it is gone. No line is written for them. A seat's turn and its lines are separate tasks; its controller batches them into one run and one change. talk: a line task is listed always for a person's line, else when a roll falls under the persona's talk.

  • Open: the roll: a hash of the seat and given, as in the code (rolls), or a seed in deal.
  • Open: who gets a person's whole-table line: the next AI seat after the speaker, as in the code (its next), or one picked by the roll.
  • Open: a person alone with AI seats: 30 s too, or a longer at. In the code, none.

Answers. apply lands a move only if base is still the turn and the dealer says it is legal; a stale or illegal change is refused, whoever sent it. A person sees why at once. For an AI seat the ledger counts a try and the turn is due again; at 3 tries, or when the run failed or the wallet would not start it, the fallback plays. A refused change lands nothing: not its line, not its reaction, not its thinking. A move that lands takes its reaction and its line with it.

Time. The clock's timeout, with its base still the turn, plays the seat's fallback; stale, it is refused. No rule reads a clock: the time is the store's at.

Members and controllers

One controller per member: the opener and each friend on their pages; each AI seat, the dealer and the clock in the room. A person's screen shows every task ("your turn", with its at); people also act unasked: chat, react, buy in, leave, next.

Presence

thinking is presence while a run goes, seen by all, never state. A run commits as soon as it ends. A screen may type a fresh line out.

System members

The dealer is a script in the room that holds the deck: on deal it shuffles and commits the hole cards and the hand, on board the street's cards. act (legal moves, pots, the showdown) stays pure in apply and deals from no deck. The clock wakes on the room's alarm at its next task's at and commits timeout with that turn as base.

Agent clients

AI seats run in the room, beside the store, with their wallets. The renderer gives the world's actions as the only tools, and thinking as text. A seat uses Ponjo's key, or that of the visitor who brought it, held by the room as rooms says. The owner always pays; the wallet's policy on from says whose lines start a run. A turn it will not start is answered as not started and falls back; the breaker counts no turns. Capacity: one queue per key in the room, turns before lines.

Differs

  • Its room's (rooms).
  • The dealer keeps no deck: for deal and board it draws from a fresh shuffle of the cards not yet out (draw, preview/poker-dealer.js); its view lists every card out, and apply checks its cards are new and enough. Its task is deal:<n>, n counting deals, a hand or none for want of chips.
  • An AI seat's program waits its persona's pace (preview/game-tables.js) after the model answers, so its change lands that much later.
