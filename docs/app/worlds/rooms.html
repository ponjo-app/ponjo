Game rooms

How Ponjo's games (poker, werewolf, spy) keep a table: an authoritative room, the usual model for turn-based games with hidden information. Each is a store of the runtime; its rules are each game's.

  • A room per table, a Cloudflare Durable Object on the relay: a Table (relay/table.js) in the relay deployed as ponjo-hackathon at infer.ponjo.app, beside the ANTHROPIC_API_KEY it runs the AI seats on. It holds { rev, state } in its storage, runs the world's apply on the runtime's core (preview/game-tables.js), and gives each client only read(member). No player's page holds the state: a refresh, a closed page or the opener leaving loses nothing, and no player, the opener included, can read another's cards or role. Its storage also keeps the members' tokens (hashed), what it spent this hour and a hash of the opener's address (for its rate); a table no page has been at for a day is deleted.

  • Clients connect to the room by WebSocket: people's pages, AI seats, the dealer. The room tells a client when its view has changed; the client reads again. Each page is one seat's client (sit, preview/game-kit.js, which holds what the three games share): the opener's keeps the table's secret, whose hash names the room, and plays as the host; a friend joins by the link.

  • Identity. The room gives each member a token when they sit. by is the member whose token sent the change. A page that comes back with its token is in its seat again; one without is a new member.

  • Leaving. The room is itself a member that watches presence: a player whose page has been gone for RECONNECT_MS it commits as leave, and the rules play the fallback on their turns.

  • Time. The clock is a member that runs in the room: the room's alarm wakes it at its next task's at, and also when a page has been gone RECONNECT_MS and every 30 s while an AI seat has a task.

  • Secrets. The dealer runs in the room; the deck leaves it only in the dealer's changes.

  • AI seats run in the room, beside the store, so no page is ever handed an AI seat's view: whoever's key one uses could otherwise read its cards. The key is Ponjo's, which never leaves the relay, or a visitor's own. A visitor's key is their wallet's: their page gives it to the room of a table they opened, which holds it in memory only, uses it only for the AI seats they brought, and sends it to no client. It is not kept at rest. A room that is evicted forgets it: the AI seats on it wait until the visitor's page comes back and gives it again, and their turns take the fallback at their deadlines meanwhile. Keeping a key at rest needs a master secret the owner sets, to encrypt it with; keeping it across tables would need accounts, which the games do not have. A visitor's key is Anthropic's, or one for an OpenAI-compatible API at a known provider's host only (OPENAI_HOSTS, relay/table.js), so the room is no proxy: a base URL elsewhere is refused when the key is given.

  • Spending. Each room has its own budget and rate, on Ponjo's key and on each visitor's, so no room spends more than its owner set: at most 300 model calls a clock hour on Ponjo's key and 600 on a visitor's (RUNS_AN_HOUR, OWN_RUNS_AN_HOUR, relay/wrangler.toml); past that, the AI seats' turns take the fallback until the next hour, and the pages say until when. Each call on Ponjo's key also counts against the address that opened the table, at most 60 a minute (INFER), and an address opens at most 60 lines to tables a minute (SEATS).

  • Presence goes to the room, which passes it on as each view's seen allows, and keeps none.

  • Deploys. A page that cannot reach the room says so and tries again, so pages published before the relay is deployed wait for it.

Differs

  • The room sends a page its whole view each time it changes, with the presence it may see, instead of telling it to read again: pages have no wait, and the worlds no past.
  • The clock and the dealer have no controllers: the room lands their due tasks itself, at once or on its alarm.
  • The AI seats share no queue per key: each calls the model on its own.
  • A visitor cannot set their key's budget or rate; they are the relay's.
