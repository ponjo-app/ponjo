Paper and ink

Ponjo's design system. The page is quiet so the writing and the buddies stand out: a warm grey ramp, one ink, and colour only where it means something.

This page is rendered with the app's own web/app.css. Change the system there and here together.

Rules for changing the UI

For anyone, person or agent, who changes how the app looks.

  1. Reuse before adding. Use an existing class (.pj-primary, .pj-secondary, .pj-icon-btn, .pj-input, .pj-segmented, .pj-switch, .pj-radio, .pj-panel…) before writing a new rule.
  2. Colours are tokens. No hex, rgb(), oklch(), black or white outside the token blocks at the top of app.css. A colour with a new meaning gets a token there, and a line on this page, first.
  3. Colour means something. Grey for the UI; ink for the one primary action and for what is on or selected; a buddy's own colour only for that buddy; red for danger and deletions (a buddy's deletions are grey); green for insertions and online; yellow for commented text; blue only for focus, text selection, found text and links.
  4. Sizes come from the scales. Font sizes 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 20, 24px; weights 400, 500, 600; radii 0, 4, 6, 10, 14px or full; spacing 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48px; control heights 24, 28, 32px (36 and 40 for bars and big buttons).
  5. One of each control. Icon buttons are .pj-icon-btn: 28px, 32 in the header, 24 inside a row. Menu and list rows share one size, set once in app.css: 28px tall, 8px side padding, radius 6, 13px; grey on hover, --active when selected or current.
  6. One primary button per view. Everything else is secondary, ghost or a link button.
  7. Sentence case. Labels and headings are never all caps.
  8. Both themes. Check light and dark; tokens flip on their own, so a change that needs a dark-only override is usually using the wrong token.

Buddies

Each buddy has its own colour, the one in its face (see Avatars). It is set as --buddy on whatever belongs to that buddy: its @chip, edits, flag and the card it is working on. A run's edits, shown from the count under its reply, are a revision in the text: new text in its colour, removed text grey and struck through where it was.

Type

Inter for the UI, with each platform's own Chinese font behind it (PingFang SC, Microsoft YaHei UI, Noto Sans CJK SC; Songti SC, Noto Serif CJK SC for serif). The Markdown editor's page font (sans, serif or mono, set in that view) and its sizes in em are the writer's, not the UI's. A text file that is not Markdown shows as it is, in --mono at 14px by default (a size from 12 to 20 in its plugin).

Save state

The toolbar says the doc's one save state, .pj-save, after its spacer: the first that holds of Not saved, Offline, Read-only, Saving and Saved, never two. 12px; Saved and Saving in --text-faint, Saved with a check; Offline and Read-only in --text-soft; Not saved in --red, 500, with the alert icon and why, cut off at 40ch. A doc that cannot be edited here (a teammate who can only read) shows no formatting tools and no Comment bubble, rather than tools that do nothing; what only reads stays: the outline, the word count, comments and history. For a teammate who can only read, the comments have no reply box, Resolve, Reopen or Undo, and history has no Restore.

Fields and choices

14 px

A stepper is a .pj-segmented with .pj-stepper: − value +, for a whole number in a range. A view's or rule's settings are rows of a switch, a segmented choice or a stepper; the first row of a group has a heading, .pj-setting-group: 12px, semibold, faint.

Settings with a page of their own

A group of settings with its own page, such as a view or a rule, is a row in a list: its name, what it is and whether it is on, the whole row one button with a chevron. Its page starts with .pj-back, the same Back as the history's.

One of several, with its settings

When exactly one of several is picked, such as the agent that runs every buddy, each is a row of .pj-choices: a .pj-radio (ink when picked), its name and what it is, the whole row its label. The picked one is a resting card (--shadow) holding its settings, in .pj-choice-detail under its name, so they read as its own and not as the next choice; the others stay closed rows. A secret, such as an API key, shows only its last 4 characters after dots; a click empties the field for a new one. Test runs the agent once on an empty doc and says under its buttons whether it works, or what it printed when it failed, in red. Where the agents can't be changed, as in the browser preview, a sentence saying why takes the intro's place and the section is shown as it is, read only: its controls disabled, radios and switches at the 0.45 opacity of a disabled button, fields as a read-only .pj-input.

Runs: a run's trace

Under the agents, Trace runs is a switch, off by default. While it is on, the owner's toolbar has Runs (the code icon), and a buddy's reply whose run left a trace has a Trace link button in its head. Runs takes the doc's place as the history does: the same bar with Back, the same list on the right (a row per run: the buddy, Failed in red or Stopped in grey on a .pj-version-tag, when and how long, the comment it answered), and the selected run as a page: its title, a line of facts in --text-soft, then parts that open and close, each a .pj-trace-part whose chevron turns: the prompt, each step (its calls in a few words, how long and its tokens on the right), the output, its changes to the doc in red and green, its lines in the comments. Text the run was given or wrote is .pj-trace-text: 12px mono on --bg-soft, a model's thinking in --text-soft; labels inside a part are .pj-setting-group.

Lists: current is ink on grey

A file clicked is current at once; until its doc is shown, the page it leaves fades to 0.6 opacity (after 0.1s, so a quick switch does not flicker) and the cursor shows progress.

A file or folder git ignores (build output, installed packages) is dimmed to --text-faint, not hidden. A filter (Mod+P) finds the files it ignores too, dimmed and after the rest in each folder, but does not look inside a folder it ignores; Enter opens the first match.

A folder window with no doc shows only its sidebar and, in the page, .pj-folder-empty: "No docs here yet." in .pj-empty, then New doc (primary) and New folder (secondary).

A sidebar is resized by dragging its edge: the cursor turns to col-resize and the edge shows a 2px --text-faint line; double-click the edge for the default width back.

Team panel

The Team button opens an .pj-panel, 360px: a head with its title and, for the owner of a project, Open room, the panel's one primary button, while no room is open; once one is, Copy link, Lock (or Unlock) and Close room (secondary, Close room red). Then .pj-people-rows in groups headed .pj-setting-group: you, Players (Players · locked while locked), AI buddies, ending with New AI buddy. A player's row says Can read or Can edit, greyed while away; its card has Read or Edit (a .pj-segmented) and Kick (secondary, red). A player's page in a browser first asks their name in a card in the middle of the page; a page turned away shows only why, in that card.

Status bar

The plain view runs edge to edge; while comments show beside it, a hairline --border on its right edge is where its text ends, lines that do not wrap scrolling inside it.

The plain view has a status bar under the page: .pj-statusbar, 28px, 12px text in --text-soft on --bg-soft, a hairline --border on top. The caret's place is on the left; on the right text buttons, 22px, grey on hover and ink on grey while pressed or open, and a menu above them in the .pj-block-menu style. Line numbers, whitespace marks (dots for spaces, arrows for tabs) are --text-faint; indent guides are --border-strong.

Find bar

Mod+F opens .pj-find at the page's top right, staying there as the page scrolls: a floating card like the link popover (radius 10, --shadow-raised), its field the link popover's, then the count, then Previous, Next and Close as icon buttons; Replace adds a row with Replace and Replace all, both secondary. Every match is --accent-soft, the current one the stronger blue of selected commented text, as the sidebar's filter marks its matches.

Links to the project's files

In a comment, a file's path in the project, as Copy Path writes it, is a link (.pj-file-link) as links in the doc are: --accent, underlined. In the link popover, what is typed that is not a URL lists the matching files under the field in the @ menu's style (.pj-mentions): the file icon, its name, its path under it in --text-soft; the arrows pick one, Enter or a click links it by its path from the doc.

The rules are in docs/architecture.md.
