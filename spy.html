The table

    Who's the Spy

    Everyone gets a secret word. Most share one; the spies, one at five or six players and two at seven or eight, get another, close to it. No one knows which they have. Each round, everyone describes their word in one clue, never saying it, talks it over for a minute and a half, then all vote out the player they think is a spy. The civilians win when the spies are out; the spies, when they make it to the last three.

    Use your own key

    The AI players run on Claude Haiku 5.5, on Ponjo. With your own key, they run on the model you pick.

    A base URL at OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, Together, DeepSeek, Mistral, Fireworks, Google's Gemini or Nebius Token Factory: your table calls no other host.

    Model

    Your key stays in this browser. While you play, your table on Ponjo's server holds it in memory only, never stored and never shown to anyone at the table, to call api.anthropic.com for your AI players. Each AI player's turn is one call billed to your key.
